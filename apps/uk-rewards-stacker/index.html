How stacking works
  • Only one cashback portal counts per purchase: the last one you click.
  • If you pay with a gift card (e.g. Freecash), card-linked rewards like Airtime don't trigger, but the card you buy the gift card with still earns points.
  • Revolut offers only pay when the Revolut card pays, so they can't combine with another card's points.
  • Loyalty schemes like Nectar stack with everything.
  • Rates are Claude's estimates and change often. Check in the app before you buy.
