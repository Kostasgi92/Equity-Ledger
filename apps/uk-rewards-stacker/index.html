The seven layers

Every reward sits on one layer of a purchase. You get at most one offer per layer, and some layers block each other. The optimiser tries every legal combination and keeps the one that pays most.

Clash rules the optimiser applies

  • One portal only. TopCashback, Quidco, Avios eStore, Revolut Shops and the rest all use a tracking cookie; the last one you click before checkout takes the credit.
  • Gift card in, card-linked out. If a Freecash or JamDoughnut gift card pays the retailer, Airtime Rewards, Amex Offers and bank offers never see a card payment at that retailer.
  • Gift card still earns card points. You buy the gift card with a card, so Virgin Points, Avios or card cashback land on the gift card purchase. Check your card's terms: a few exclude gift-card merchants.
  • Gift card and portal usually stack. Most portals pay on gift-card-funded orders. Some retailers exclude them; the offer's caveat says so when known.
  • Payment-locked offers need that card. Revolut Shops, Revolut Merchant Rewards and Amex Offers only pay when that card pays. They can't combine with a different card's points, or with a gift card.
  • PayPal checkout hides the merchant. PayPal+ boosts need PayPal checkout. The card funding PayPal usually still earns, but card-linked offers rarely track through PayPal.
  • Codes can void cashback. A discount code not listed on the portal can cancel the portal's cashback. The best stack flags this when it uses both.
  • Loyalty always stacks. Nectar, Clubcard and retailer apps sit on top of everything.

Your platforms at a glance

Freecash. Gift-card cashback marketplace: buy a retailer gift card at face value, get coins back (1,000 coins ≈ $1), redeemable for PayPal, Amazon vouchers or crypto. 40+ UK retailers, e.g. Sainsbury's ~2%, ASDA/Morrisons ~1.9%, M&S ~4.9%, John Lewis ~2.8%, adidas ~7.9%, JD Sports ~5.3%, Currys ~4%.

Avios app. Avios eStore is a click-through portal (roughly 1–12 Avios per £1). Avios in-store works on a registered card at listed retailers.

PayPal+. 1 point per £10 via PayPal checkout, more on the PayPal debit card. The value is in boosted offers on rotating retailers (up to ~10 points/£1). 1,000 points = £10.

Revolut. Shops: click through the app and pay with Revolut for up to ~3% (cash or RevPoints). Merchant Rewards: rotating offers you activate first. Both need the Revolut card to pay.

Airtime Rewards. Card-linked: link a card once, shop normally at 150–250+ retailers, 1–10%+ back as mobile-bill credit or vouchers.

Engage. Employer benefit (Reward Gateway): discounted eGift cards at 100+ retailers, reloadable cards and click-through cashback. The eGift cards behave like a gift-card layer.

Nectar. 1 point/£1 at Sainsbury's (~0.5p each) plus personalised offers and Nectar Prices. Partners include Argos, eBay and Esso. Converts to Avios.

Virgin Atlantic credit card. Reward+ 1.5 Virgin Points/£1, Reward 0.75/£1, double on Virgin Atlantic/Holidays. It's your card-earn layer, so it stacks with portals, PayPal+ and Airtime, and with gift cards when you use it to buy them.
